Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you enquire.

Clear answers to common questions regarding MOQs, sampling, shipping ports, documentation, and payment terms.

9 questions available

What core products does Bharat Exim Traders export? +
Products

Our three primary export categories are whole & ground Indian spices, women’s ethnic and contemporary apparel, and canine walking & lifestyle accessories. Please review our specific portfolio pages for full details.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)? +
Orders

MOQs vary according to the product type, fabric availability, packaging requirements, and seasonal harvests. We maintain flexible trial-batch policies for new international buyers so you can test your market with confidence.

Can I request pre-shipment samples? +
Orders

Yes. We encourage sample evaluation before placing full production orders. Sample availability, shipping courier fees, and production lead times are confirmed individually upon receiving your brief.

Do you offer private labelling and custom packaging? +
Products

Yes. We support custom branding, woven labels, retail boxes, barcode labelling, multi-language packaging, and custom pack sizes for both wholesale distribution and direct-to-retail requirements.

Which destination countries and ports do you ship to? +
Shipping

We export worldwide through major Indian sea and air ports (including Mumbai Nhava Sheva JNPT and international air cargo terminals). We arrange shipments under FOB, CIF, or CFR terms based on your preferred logistics framework.

What are the typical lead times from order to dispatch? +
Shipping

Standard lead times range between 2 to 6 weeks depending on batch volume, custom embroidery/dyeing for apparel, agricultural milling schedules, and packaging preparation.

How do you handle certificates and customs documentation? +
Documents

We supply complete commercial documentation, including Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary certificates for spices, commercial invoice, packing list, and bill of lading tailored to your port of entry requirements.

How do I submit an enquiry and receive a formal quotation? +
Orders

You can submit your brief directly via our Contact page form or email us at bharateximtraders@gmail.com. We generate a detailed quotation specifying unit prices, lead times, packing dimensions, and delivery terms.

What are your standard payment terms? +
Orders

Payment terms are discussed transparently and agreed prior to order confirmation. Standard arrangements typically include partial deposit upon proforma invoice with balance against shipping documentation (or Irrevocable Letter of Credit for qualified large orders).
